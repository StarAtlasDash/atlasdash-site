About

AtlasDash is the analytical hub of the Star Atlas metaverse, built by Aephia Industries for anyone researching, playing, or investing in Star Atlas.

We've been tracking the Star Atlas economy since the summer of 2022. It started with the first on-chain analytics charts, then monthly economic reports, and eventually the official Star Atlas dashboards on Flipside Crypto. AtlasDash is the evolution of that work: everything we've learned about this ecosystem, brought together in one place.

Whether you're exploring trends, studying player behavior, or shaping DAC strategy, AtlasDash helps you see Star Atlas in motion.

All data is refreshed daily or faster. Free and open, for the community.
